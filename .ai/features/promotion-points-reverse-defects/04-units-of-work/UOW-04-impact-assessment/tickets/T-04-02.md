# T-04-02 — Write up what the numbers mean

## Context

T-04-01 produces counts. This ticket turns them into the one thing the owner actually needs:
whether a remediation is worth writing. ADR-04 is explicit that this slice recommends and does
not decide, and that any remediation corrects by `point_history` ADJUST entry rather than by
editing rows.

## Implementation notes

- Lead with the two counts and the clone's snapshot date. Numbers first, prose second.
- Split real organizations from demo/test ones explicitly. If every affected row is demo data,
  say so in the first paragraph and recommend fix-forward — that is the likely outcome and it
  should be easy to reach.
- If real customer cards were debited, list them: customer, invoice, points destroyed, date.
  A remediation, if one follows, needs that list as its input, and producing it here means the
  remediation feature starts from evidence rather than re-deriving it.
- State the recommendation in one sentence with its reason, and mark plainly that the decision
  is the owner's.
- Note anything the queries could not measure — the #15 predicate's blind spots from T-04-01 —
  so the recommendation is not read as more complete than it is.
- Close the loop on A-06: record whether the affected data turned out to be dev-test or real,
  since that is what the assumption was pending on.
